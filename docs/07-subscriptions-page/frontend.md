# 07 Subscriptions page: frontend

Layout follows the brief's reference (Bloom's subscription page), branded for Woman Bloom.
1. **Hero:** full-bleed uploadable image with an ink gradient overlay. A rounded "SAVE 15%" badge, H1 "Subscriptions to keep you blooming", lead copy, a "Subscribe & Save" CTA that **smooth-scrolls to the product grid** on the same page, and an "Already a subscriber? Manage it here" link.
2. **Perks strip:** 4 items (icon in a pink-tint circle + line).
3. **Save 15% on all your faves:** grid of products, each with a **15% OFF badge at the top right** of the image (badge size scales by breakpoint), rating, title, short copy, subscribed price + struck-through one-time price, "Subscribe now" (→ PDP with Subscribe preselected).
4. **Subscribing has its perks:** comparison table, Subscription ✓ vs One-time ✕, with a CTA.
5. **The results speak for themselves:** 3 testimonial cards with the product row (→ PDP, Subscribe preselected). The section background runs pink at the top and fades to white at the bottom, continuing the gradient from the section above.
6. **Got questions?** FAQ accordion (same animation as the PDP).
