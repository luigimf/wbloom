# 08 About, FAQ & contact: frontend

- **About:** founder story and brand mission as alternating image/text rows. The image keeps its position and size when "Read more" expands: the container grows downward with a smooth height animation, and the image never scales. Three lines are shown collapsed, and the third line is always fully visible.
- **Who we're for:** audience portraits (4:5) with short copy.
- **FAQ:** category tabs (Products, Subscriptions, Orders & delivery…) and accordion items (same animation as the PDP).
- **Contact:** form (name, email, order number optional, message) with validation, a success state, and the support email.
