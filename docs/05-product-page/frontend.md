# 05 Product page: frontend

- **Gallery:** large square main image with a thumbnail row underneath, scaled so the row matches the main image's width. Clicking a thumbnail swaps the main image.
- **Buy box:** title, rating, price, short description, flavour selector, **purchase plan** (One-time purchase is the default; Subscribe & save 15% with a 30/45/60-day frequency), quantity, Add to bag.
  - Arriving from the Subscriptions page preselects **Subscribe**.
  - Arriving from a "Subscribe & save" email link (`?plan=subscribe`) preselects Subscribe.
- **Accordions** (Ingredients, How to use, Shipping & returns, FAQ): smooth height + opacity animation (0.38s) and a plus icon that rotates to a cross. One open at a time.
- **Three things we stand by** standards strip; its heading wraps below desktop.
- **Pairs well with:** responsive grid (3 desktop / 2 tablet / 2 mobile) of standard product cards.
- Every product image anywhere on the page links to its PDP.
